# Studio branding integration

The brand design workspace now lives in the adjacent `../cmmf-branding` folder. Its current guide and usage rules are under `docs/brand/`; its full kit and design history are under `artifacts/`.

Studio keeps its runtime dependencies here: `src/assets/brand/`, `src/brand.css` and `public/brand/`. The application builds independently of the sibling branding workspace.

Source Sans 3 is the operational default. Quattrocento is reserved for dedicated reading content, including the canvas guide. Reem Kufi appears in the logo and selected brand headings. Editable descriptions and short hints remain Source Sans 3.

When updating the identity, review the canonical rules in the branding workspace, then copy the approved assets and tokens into the app. Header assets may trim transparent canvas padding to align the visible artwork; preserve the actual symbol and type paths.
